\section{Profile}
\footnotesize
Quantitative researcher with \textbf{5 years} of work on models that break under the usual assumptions:
discretisation schemes with proven convergence, multi-parameter estimation from discrete data, sensitivity
analysis, and \textbf{risk-aware learning} (AISTATS). Eight peer-reviewed papers. I write \textbf{Python}
daily and ship tools other people use. I want that same rigour applied to \textbf{product data}: pipelines
you can trust, metrics you can defend, and decisions rather than theorems.
\normalsize
