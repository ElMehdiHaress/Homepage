\section{Profile}
\footnotesize
Researcher with \textbf{5 years} of work on \textbf{how a state evolves over time} and how to learn it from
data: stochastic dynamical models, \textbf{estimation of several parameters from discrete, noisy
observations}, convergence and \textbf{stability guarantees}, and \textbf{risk-aware learning} robust to
heavy tails (2 \emph{AISTATS} papers). Eight peer-reviewed papers; I have driven my own research from the
question to the published result, and released the code. I want to bring this to world models of patient
health, where calibration and robustness decide whether a model can be trusted in the clinic.
\normalsize
