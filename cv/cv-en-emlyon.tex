\section{Profile}
\footnotesize
Applied mathematician (PhD, Université Paris-Saclay; engineer, CentraleSupélec) with \textbf{five years of
university teaching} in probability, statistics, PDEs and stochastic finance, from first-year undergraduates
to M2, in \textbf{French and English}, including students outside mathematics (economics, engineering).
I teach mathematics through the questions it answers: real data sets, simulation in Python, and
student projects on real-world problems. Research in stochastic modelling, statistical inference and
risk-aware learning (8 peer-reviewed papers, including 2 at \emph{AISTATS}), with ongoing work alongside
industrial partners at Inria.
\normalsize
