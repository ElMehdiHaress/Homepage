Dear Hiring Team,

Your posting does not open with a stack. It opens with \textbf{reasoning, drive, and team spirit}. That is why I am writing. I have spent five years on problems where the usual assumptions fail, and the work was to decide what still holds, what is noise, and what would break a conclusion. That is the same muscle as asking whether a metric is trustworthy before anyone builds a dashboard on it.

I am a PhD in applied mathematics (Paris-Saclay / CentraleSupélec) and a research engineer at Inria. My papers are about \textbf{numerical schemes with proven convergence}, joint estimation of several parameters from discrete observations, and the \textbf{sensitivity of a model to a change in its specification}. Two of them, at \emph{AISTATS}, bring spectral risk measures into statistical learning under heavy tails. I write \textbf{Python} every day and I publish the code. I do not arrive as a product-analytics specialist with a year of Looker behind me. I arrive as someone who can sit with a messy pipeline, name the assumption, and test it.

I also ship. On my own time I have built and run \textbf{Nerduncover}, a multiplayer web game; a production site and back-office for a real client, \href{https://hanabellaspa.pro}{hanabellaspa.pro}; and my own site, \href{https://elmehdiharess.org}{elmehdiharess.org}. That work taught me data models, authentication, and putting a tool in someone else's hands. It is the closest thing I have to ``internal data products'': not a research notebook, a thing other people use.

What I want now is a small, talent-dense team where the output is a decision, not a theorem. I am based in France, and I work in English as a daily language after two years in the United Kingdom.

I would welcome the tests. If a problem is unfamiliar, that is usually the interesting part.

Yours sincerely,
